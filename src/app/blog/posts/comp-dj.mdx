## Description

In this company, I was responsible for designing Instagram feed posts, video thumbnails, and Instagram Stories to support the company's digital branding and
publication needs. Each design was developed with careful attention to visual consistency, layout composition, design element selection, and information hierarchy
to ensure that the content was both engaging and easy for the audience to understand.

Throughout the design process, I consistently applied the company's four primary brand colors as the main visual palette to
maintain a strong brand identity and create a cohesive, professional, and visually appealing social media presence. This approach helped ensure that all
digital content aligned with the company's brand character and communication goals across various digital platforms.

## Feed Design

This Instagram feed design was created with a strong emphasis on visual consistency and brand identity through the use of
a color palette that aligns with the company's character and values. The typography was carefully selected to be modern, clear, and
highly readable, ensuring a comfortable and effective information delivery experience for the audience.

The layout was structured with careful consideration of visual hierarchy, spacing, and compositional balance to present information in a clear,
engaging, and visually appealing manner. In addition, graphic elements and supporting imagery were strategically incorporated to create a professional, communicative,
and cohesive feed appearance across all content, reinforcing the brand's presence and consistency on social media platforms.

## Instagram Stories Design & Video Thumbnail

The Instagram Story designs were created with an engaging and informative visual approach by utilizing a combination of colors, typography, and
layouts that aligned with the brand identity. This approach ensured that information was delivered clearly, effectively, and in a visually appealing manner while
maintaining consistency across the company's digital communication channels.

The video thumbnail designs were developed by emphasizing compelling visual elements, strategic color usage, and
eye-catching typography to capture audience attention and encourage engagement. In addition, each thumbnail was designed to maintain brand consistency and
strengthen the visual identity of the company's video content across digital platforms.
