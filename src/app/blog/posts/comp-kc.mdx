## Description

In this company, I was responsible for designing Instagram feed posts, video thumbnails, and Instagram Stories to support the company's digital branding
and publication needs. The design process focused on maintaining visual consistency, organizing layouts effectively, selecting appropriate design elements,
and applying a clear information hierarchy to ensure that the content was both engaging and easy for the audience to understand.

For every design, I consistently utilized the company's four primary brand colors as the main visual palette to preserve its visual identity and strengthen brand recognition.
This approach helped create a cohesive, professional, and visually appealing social media presence that aligned with the company's character and communication objectives
across digital platforms.

## Feed Design

This Instagram feed design was developed with a strong emphasis on visual consistency and brand identity through the use of a color palette that aligns with the company's 
character and values. The typography was carefully selected to be modern, clear, and highly readable, enhancing the audience's ability to absorb information comfortably and effectively.

The layout was structured with careful consideration of visual hierarchy, spacing, and compositional balance to ensure that information was communicated clearly and presented in an engaging manner. 
Additionally, graphic elements and supporting imagery were thoughtfully incorporated to create a professional, communicative, and cohesive visual appearance across all feed content. This approach helped strengthen brand 
recognition while maintaining a consistent and visually appealing presence on social media platforms.

## WhatsApp Blast Design

This WhatsApp Blast design was created with a focus on delivering information in a concise, clear, and easily understandable manner. The use of colors, typography, and 
visual elements was carefully aligned with the brand identity to maintain a consistent and professional appearance across communication materials.

The layout was structured to highlight key information and guide the audience's attention effectively, ensuring that the message could be communicated clearly and efficiently. 
Particular consideration was given to mobile viewing, resulting in a visually appealing design that remained easy to read and engaging across various mobile devices.
