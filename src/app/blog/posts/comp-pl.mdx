## Description

In this company, I was responsible for designing Instagram feed posts and Instagram Stories to support the company's digital branding and publication efforts. The design process focused on maintaining visual consistency, 
organizing layouts effectively, selecting appropriate design elements, and applying a clear information hierarchy to ensure that content was both engaging and easy for the audience to understand.

For each design, I consistently utilized the company's four primary brand colors as the main visual palette to preserve brand identity and strengthen brand recognition. This approach helped create a cohesive, professional, 
and visually appealing social media presence while ensuring that all content remained aligned with the company's character and communication objectives across digital platforms.

## Feed Design

This Instagram feed design was developed with a strong emphasis on visual consistency and brand identity through the use of a color palette that reflects the company's character and values. The typography was carefully 
selected to be modern, clear, and easy to read, enhancing the audience's ability to absorb information comfortably and effectively.

The layout was structured with careful attention to visual hierarchy, spacing, and compositional balance to ensure that information was communicated clearly and presented in an engaging manner. Additionally, graphic elements 
and supporting imagery were thoughtfully incorporated to create a professional, communicative, and cohesive visual appearance across all feed content. This approach helped strengthen brand recognition while maintaining 
a consistent and visually appealing presence on social media platforms.

The Instagram Story designs were created with an engaging and informative visual approach by combining colors, typography, and layouts that aligned with the brand identity. This design strategy ensured that information

Careful attention was given to the arrangement of visual elements and content hierarchy to enhance readability and audience engagement. As a result, the designs not only conveyed key messages efficiently but also reinforced 
the brand's visual identity and consistency throughout its social media presence.

## Presentation Deck and Zoom Virtual Background Design

The presentation deck was designed with careful consideration of layout, typography, color selection, and visual hierarchy to ensure that information could be delivered clearly, professionally, and 
effectively to the audience. Each slide was structured to enhance readability, maintain audience engagement, and support the overall communication objectives of the presentation.

The Zoom virtual background was developed with a strong emphasis on visual identity and branding through the strategic use of brand colors, logos, and graphic elements. This approach helped create a professional 
and cohesive visual appearance during virtual events while reinforcing brand recognition and maintaining consistency across digital communication channels.
