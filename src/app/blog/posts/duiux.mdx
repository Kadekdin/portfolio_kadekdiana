## Description

In this project, I designed the UI/UX for a mobile application that provides a variety of cooking recipes, helping users easily discover, save, and follow step-by-step cooking instructions. 
The design process focused on intuitive navigation, information accessibility, and overall user convenience to ensure a seamless experience when searching for recipes that matched users' preferences and needs.

The interface was designed with a clean, simple, and user-friendly layout, supported by appealing food imagery and a clear information hierarchy. In addition, I developed wireframes, user flows, and interactive prototypes 
to validate the usability of each feature and ensure an efficient user journey. This approach helped create an engaging and effective user experience for exploring and interacting with a wide range of recipes.
