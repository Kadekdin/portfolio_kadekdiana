## Description

In the EC Movie Time program, which featured a horror theme, I was responsible for creating various digital assets to support the event's operational and promotional needs, including MC cue cards, promotional thumbnails,
and participant check-out attendance materials. Each design was developed to align with the horror concept through the strategic use of colors, typography, and visual elements that created a mysterious atmosphere while
capturing participants' attention.

Beyond serving as informational materials, these digital assets also played a key role in strengthening the event's visual identity. By maintaining consistency across all design outputs, the assets contributed to a more
professional, engaging, and cohesive event experience throughout the program.
