## Description

In this program, I was responsible for designing event posters as informational and promotional materials to communicate key event details in an engaging and effective manner. 
The design process focused on layout composition, typography, and visual elements that aligned with the event concept, helping to enhance promotional appeal and audience engagement.

In addition, I documented the event through photography and videography to support publication needs, organizational archives, and activity records. The documentation process ensured that important moments were 
captured professionally and could be utilized for reporting purposes, future promotional activities, and content publication across the organization's digital media platforms.

## Photography & Photo Editing

The photo editing process for this project was designed to capture the warm and natural atmosphere of a rural morning. The editing focused on adjusting lighting, color grading, and 
tonal balance to emphasize the sunrise ambiance, creating a sense of calmness, freshness, and positive energy throughout the images.

Warm color tones, including golden yellow and soft orange hues, were carefully applied to enhance the early morning atmosphere and reinforce the natural character of the environment. This approach helped create visually 
appealing photographs that conveyed a lively, authentic, and comfortable impression while effectively reflecting the mood and activities captured during the event.
