## Description

This SME operates in the culinary industry, focusing on the production and sale of various cake products. Within the SME digitalization project, I contributed by managing digital content creation, 
including social media design and video editing, to support product promotion, strengthen the brand’s visual identity, and increase marketing reach through digital platforms such as Instagram.

## Feed Design

This Instagram feed design was created to support the promotion of a bakery business through a visually appealing, informative, and consistent layout aligned with the brand identity. The design utilizes a color palette 
that reflects the brand’s character, along with clear and readable typography to ensure effective communication of product information.

The layout was structured to highlight key content and featured products, supported by a well-organized visual hierarchy and balanced composition. This approach ensures that the content remains engaging, 
easy to understand, and visually attractive for the target audience.

## Editing Video

In this SME digitalization project, I was responsible for video editing to support product promotion and social media publication. The editing process involved structuring the video flow, adding transitions, 
text overlays, music, and color adjustments to create content that was engaging, informative, and effective in attracting potential customers.

In addition, the videos were designed to align with the business's visual identity and serve as effective digital marketing materials, enhancing the overall appeal and professionalism of the brand's online presence.
