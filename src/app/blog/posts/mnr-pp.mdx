## Description

The video editing process for the speaker session at the Minora event was developed with a fun, interactive, and energetic concept to reflect the atmosphere of the activity involving high school students.
The editing focused on creating a dynamic flow through the selection of the best footage, along with the addition of transitions, text, music, and supporting visual effects to make the content more engaging and lively.

In addition to highlighting the speaker’s content, the video also emphasized participant interactions and enthusiasm throughout the event, effectively showcasing an active, educational, and enjoyable atmosphere.
The goal was to produce a video that was not only informative but also capable of increasing audience engagement through clear, communicative, and visually appealing storytelling.
