## Description

This personal photography project explores the harmonious relationship between feminine elegance and the natural beauty of flowers as the main visual elements.
Through the combination of expression, composition, color, and floral details, the project aims to create soft, elegant, and artistic visual storytelling.

In this project, I was fully responsible for the entire process, from concept development and photo shooting to post-production and editing. The editing process
involved color correction, lighting adjustments, and tonal refinement to enhance a graceful, natural, and aesthetic atmosphere, resulting in visuals that effectively
convey emotion and storytelling in a more profound way.

## Photography & Photo Editing

The photo editing process in this project combined both cold and warm tone concepts, adjusted according to the lighting characteristics of each image. Warm tones were
used to emphasize natural light and create a soft, elegant atmosphere, while cool tones were applied to evoke a calm, dramatic, and emotional mood.

Sky scenery and floral elements were used as the main visual focus to strengthen the natural essence of the composition. Careful adjustments in color, contrast, lighting,
and detail were applied to achieve a balanced harmony between the subject, sky, and flowers, resulting in aesthetically pleasing visuals with strong character and a well-defined emotional atmosphere.
